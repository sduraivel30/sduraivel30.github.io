more: >
  We recorded intraoperatively with high-density micro-electrocorticography (μECoG), achieving roughly
  57 times higher spatial resolution and ~48% higher signal-to-noise ratio than standard macro-ECoG or
  SEEG electrodes. This higher-resolution signal improved speech decoding accuracy by about 35% relative
  to standard intracranial recordings, indicating that decoding performance for speech neuroprostheses is
  fundamentally limited by the spatial resolution of the recording interface, not just the decoding
  algorithm.
---
People who lose the ability to speak due to neurodegenerative disease could regain communication through neural speech prostheses that decode intended speech directly from brain signals. This paper asks how much of that decoding bottleneck comes from the coarseness of standard intracranial recordings, using high-density micro-ECoG recorded during intraoperative speech production.
