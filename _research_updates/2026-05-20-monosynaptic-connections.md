The functional organization of the human cortex has been mapped in detail, but the anatomical wiring linking these areas is much less understood. In this project, we used simultaneous electrical stimulation and fMRI (es-fMRI) in patients undergoing intracranial monitoring to trace direct, monosynaptic anatomical connections between cortical sites across the whole brain — including a link between the fusiform face area and the temporoparietal junction, two regions both implicated in social cognition.
