more: >
  Combining data across patients is difficult because of differences in neuroanatomy, electrode placement,
  and sparse sampling of the targeted cortex. Using canonical correlation analysis with high-density
  micro-ECoG, we found shared latent neural dynamics across patients that preserved fine-grained,
  micro-scale speech information. Decoding models trained on this shared latent space outperformed
  patient-specific models, suggesting a path toward speech neuroprostheses that need less
  patient-specific training data.

Speech brain-computer interfaces (BCIs) could restore communication for people with neuromotor disorders, but current approaches require large amounts of patient-specific training data collected over long periods. This project asks whether aligning neural data from different patients into a shared latent space can allow a speech BCI to be trained across patients rather than starting from scratch for each one.
