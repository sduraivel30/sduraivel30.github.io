more: >
  During planning, different levels of speech units (e.g., phonemes, syllables) are represented discretely
  across distinct prefrontal sites. These planned units are then dynamically integrated across cortex to
  guide execution. During execution itself, speech motor regions generate continuous activity that reflects
  both the discrete planned units and the transitions between them — a rapid shift from discrete planning
  representations to continuous motor sequences that may help explain how speaking feels effortless.
---
Although speaking recruits a distributed network of brain regions, it has been unclear how the brain coordinates planning a sentence with actually executing the movements to speak it. Using the high spatial and temporal resolution of intracranial recordings, this dissertation-based project identifies distinct neural mechanisms for speech planning versus speech execution.
